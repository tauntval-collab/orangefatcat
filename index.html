What I make

Weapon auras

Layered particle emitters fitted to the mesh — flame, lightning, frost, glow, sparkle. Built to read clearly at gameplay distance.

Swing trails

Trails attached and tuned per weapon so swings feel fast and weighty, with colour matched to the blade.

Beams & lights

Beams, energy arcs and point lights for hero weapons that need to stand out from the rest of the rack.

Abilities & impacts

Character auras, explosions, hit effects, portals and shields for combat and anime-style games, tuned to read in the middle of a fight.

Themed & rarity sets

Matching looks across a whole world or event — beach, cherry blossom, seasonal drops — with intensity that scales by rarity.

How a commission works

  1. Brief

    DM me your meshes or ability list, the theme or references, and how many weapons or effects you need done.

  2. Sample

    I do one weapon or effect first so we lock the style before the full batch.

  3. Batch & revisions

    The rest of the set gets built to match. You review in Studio and call out changes.

  4. Delivery

    You get a model file with every effect already attached and named — drop it in and it works.
